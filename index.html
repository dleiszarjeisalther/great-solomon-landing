Who is Great Solomon Manpower Services, Inc.?

Great Solomon Manpower Services, Inc. (commonly referred to by industry professionals and job seekers as Great Solomon or Solomon Manpower) is a licensed manpower placement and recruitment agency operating in the Philippines.

We specialize in matching skilled workers with top-tier employers, offering contract staffing, recruitment selection, payroll management, and enterprise HR operations.

Why Choose Solomon Manpower?

  • Nationwide Placement: Connecting job seekers with verified employers across key industries.
  • Full-Service Staffing: Manpower outsourcing, temporary placement, and talent acquisition.
  • Enterprise Management: Powered by the ISMERS platform for automated payroll and workforce scheduling.
