# ISMERS module catalog

## Core Transaction 1 — Client Acquisition, Recruitment & Deployment

- Client Management
- Applicant Registration and Profiling
- Recruitment and Selection
- Job Order Management
- Deployment and Assignment

Typical flow: client need → job order → applicant profile → recruitment and selection → deployment or assignment.

## Core Transaction 2 — Human Resource Information & Operations (HRIS)

- Employee Information Management (HRIS)
- Timekeeping and Attendance
- Leave and Absence Management
- Payroll and Compensation
- Performance Management

Typical flow: employee master record → attendance and leave → payroll and compensation → performance history.

## Core Transaction 3 — Employee Development, Compliance & Benefits

- Training and Development
- Document and Contract Management
- Government Contribution & Compliance
- Benefits and Loans Management
- Separation and Exit Clearance

## Core Transaction 4 — Governance, Safety & System Administration

- Health, Safety, and Welfare
- Legal and Compliance
- System Administration and Security
- Reports, Analytics, and Dashboards
- Asset and Equipment Issuance Tracker

## Financial Management

## Cross-module relationships

| Source | Downstream or related use |
|---|---|
| CRM | Client acquisition, client history, job orders |
| Client Management | Job orders, recruitment demand, deployment |
| Applicant Profiling | Recruitment and selection, employee onboarding |
| Employee HRIS | Attendance, leave, payroll, performance, benefits |
| Job Orders and Deployment | Assignment, fleet/transport needs, client reporting |
| Training and Compliance | Employee eligibility, deployment readiness, audit records |
| Procurement and Inventory | Assets, equipment issuance, operations |
| Finance | Payroll support, collections, disbursements, budgets, reporting |
| Operational modules | BI dashboards, KPIs, forecasts, decision support |
